How It Works

What does the blur circle mean?

When your camera focuses on the subject, objects that are closer or farther away from the focus plane are projected as circles instead of sharp points on the sensor.

The further the background is from the focus plane, and the wider the aperture, the larger this circle becomes.

This calculator gives you a physical measurement of that blur directly on the sensor, making it easier to compare different lenses and shooting setups.

Blur Circle Formula

c = blur circle diameter, f = focal length, N = f-number, s = subject distance, b = background distance from camera.
